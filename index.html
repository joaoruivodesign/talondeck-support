TalonDeck Support

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Need help with TalonDeck? You’re in the right place. TalonDeck is a personal media backlog tracker for movies, TV shows, books, and games.

Contact

The fastest way to reach us is by email: talondeck@gmail.com. We read every message and aim to reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a card?

Tap the + button on any pile, search for a movie, TV show, book, or game, pick the result, and drop it into the pile you want.

How do the piles work?

Each card moves through your deck:

  • Up Next — things you plan to start
  • In Play — what you’re currently watching, reading, or playing (one active card per suit)
  • Completed — everything you’ve finished
  • Burn — things you’ve decided to give up on, guilt-free

Drag a card from one pile to another to move it.

How do I track progress?

Tap a card to open its details, where you can update progress such as minutes watched, episodes seen, or pages read, and rank it once finished.

Where is my data stored?

All of your cards are stored privately on your device. TalonDeck has no accounts and does not collect personal information. See our Privacy Policy.

What is TalonDeck Pro?

Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription that unlocks unlimited cards across every pile and your full Completed and Burn history. You can use TalonDeck for free without it.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open the Profile tab and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your device, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select TalonDeck, and cancel there.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

Please email talondeck@gmail.com with as much detail as you can, including your device model and iOS version. It really helps.
